Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Lyna Kids World Studio ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Lyna Kids World YouTube channel to publish the channel's own videos.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel and set their title, description, tags and schedule;
  • add those videos to the owner's playlists and upload their captions;
  • read basic information and public statistics (views, likes) of the owner's own videos.

What the app stores

The only stored credential is the OAuth refresh token of the channel owner, kept privately by the owner. The app keeps a list of its own uploads (title, video ID, view count) to plan future videos. It does not collect, store or share any personal data of viewers, and it collects no data from children.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube to perform the actions listed above.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google security settings.
