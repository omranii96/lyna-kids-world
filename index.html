Lyna Kids World Studio

Lyna Kids World Studio is the private publishing tool of the Lyna Kids World YouTube channel: short, original songs and stories for children aged 2 to 6.

The tool is used only by the channel owner. It uploads the channel's own videos, schedules them, and reads their view counts through the YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public and does not collect any data from viewers.
